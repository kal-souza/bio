Publication

The male breadwinner norm in Brazil.
Kauany de Souza, Fabiana Rocha, and Pedro Forquesato.
Economics Bulletin, 43(4): 1759–1770, 2023.

Dissertation. Bunching estimation of the male breadwinner norm in Brazil. Using Brazilian Census data, the dissertation models the breadwinner norm as an implicit tax on women's earnings and uses bunching methods to relate the excess mass at the 50% relative-earnings threshold to labor-supply responses. It then studies robustness against alternative explanations, including coworking and self-employment, minimum-wage bunching, round-number reporting, and assortative matching.
